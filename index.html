Hoot Math: Math for Kids

Math practice for ages 5 to 10 on iPhone and iPad, with Professor Hoot. Children practice counting, adding, taking away, groups and times tables, word problems, patterns, shapes and telling time.

  • Questions change with your child's age and get harder as they improve
  • A hint after the first miss, and a clear explanation after the second
  • Questions can be read aloud
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking

The Easy level of every game is free. A one-time purchase opens Medium and Hard.
