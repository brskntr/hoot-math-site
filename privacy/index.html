Hoot Math

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy covers the Hoot Math: Math for Kids app for iPhone and iPad, made by Barış Kantar.

In short: the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We do not collect personal information from children or grown-ups through the app.

What stays on your device

The app saves the chosen age group, stars, levels and settings on your device so your child can continue later. It does not send them to us. They may be part of your own device backup, which Apple manages. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

AI-written questions and pictures

On supported devices, Apple's on-device model can write the wording of questions. The app makes the numbers and answers itself and checks the wording before showing it. If a grown-up chooses Image Playground for pictures, the app uses Apple's image feature. The app does not give your child's name or progress to these features. Grown-ups can turn both off in the settings.

Voice

Questions are read aloud with the system voice. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. We do not receive your name or payment details. Apple gives us sales and refund reports that do not identify you. See Apple's privacy policy.

Crash reports and TestFlight

If you share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us crash and usage reports. If you test the app with TestFlight, Apple also sends us the crash reports and feedback you submit.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. Write to us at any time to have the messages deleted. Please do not send a child's personal information.

Children

The app is made for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Purchases and settings sit behind a grown-ups question. If you think a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: bariskantar@gmail.com
