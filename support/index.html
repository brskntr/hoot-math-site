Support

Questions or problems? Write to bariskantar@gmail.com.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the lock at the top right, answer the grown-ups question, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

What is free, and what does the purchase open?

The Easy level of every game is free. Hoot Math Full is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It opens Medium and Hard in every game and supports Family Sharing.

How do levels work?

Each game moves at your child's own pace. Three right answers in a row on the first try move it up a level, and two misses in a row move it down.

How do I change the age or turn off the voice?

Tap the lock, answer the question, and change Age group or Read questions aloud.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which devices work?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the on-device model can write the wording of questions. On other devices the app uses its own built-in wording.
